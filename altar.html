The Workshop

Build an arrangement

Choose signs and tools from the library and set them where they belong — the room will share what convention says about each place before you commit. Nothing here is graded; everything here is teachable.

  • Pick an item from the library — the room lights up every placeable spot (toggle “show places” anytime).
  • Scroll / pinch to zoom, drag empty space to orbit, right-drag to pan, or jump with the views panel.
  • Aim at a lit marker and click to place. Shift-click places another of the same.
  • Drag a placed item to move it; R rotates, Delete removes, Ctrl+Z undoes.
  • Toggle sandbox to summarize a practice arrangement.
